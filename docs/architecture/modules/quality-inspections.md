# Daijo MES: Quality Inspections & Gates

## 1. First Piece Inspection (`FirstPieceInspection`)
- Quality gate conducted at the start of a production run.
- Acts as a required approval gate before Second Process PQC signoff.
- Tracks initial setup accuracy, dimensions, appearance, and inspector signoffs.

## 2. In-Process Quality Control (`IpqcInspection`)
- IPQC inspection recording defect rates, sample inspection rounds, and judgements.
- Hourly and lot-based sampling checks on the shop floor.
